What this survey does not say

  • 1,670 accommodations — 12% — are not listed by name. 92 names designate several distinct places: their points do not fit within 0.5°. Here, "Kampung Baru" gathers 7 accommodations spread over 24.3 degrees — several distinct places under one name. Adding them up would manufacture a false figure. They are counted in the total, never named.
  • One place can appear twice. A locality is sometimes recorded under its town name AND under that of its administrative division. This is not corrected: doing so would invent a hierarchy the data does not carry.
  • 4,729 accommodations are set aside as private and appear nowhere on this page. A private home is not an establishment: its address is a residence.
  • No prices, no availability, no reviews. This survey says what exists and where. It does not say what it is worth, or whether it is open.
