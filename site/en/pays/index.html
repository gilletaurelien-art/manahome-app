What this survey does not say

  • 440,664 accommodations are set aside as private and appear on none of these pages. A private home is not an establishment: its address is a residence.
  • 163,466 accommodations — 25% — are not listed by place name. Outside France a town name is not an identifier: it often designates several distinct places, sometimes thousands of kilometres apart. A name is kept only if all its points fit within half a degree. The rest are counted in the totals, never named.
  • 40 countries have no page, for want of a survey rich enough to carry one. They are not forgotten: they return when the harvest provides them.
  • No prices, no availability, no reviews. This survey says what exists and where. It does not say what it is worth, or whether it is open.
