The method, and its limits

A figure without its method is not a measurement, it is an assertion. Here is how this one is made, and everything it does not say.

Survey of 2026-11-11

Where the data comes from

7 open sources feed the survey. OpenStreetMap carries the largest share and covers the world; the others are national or regional registers, each bringing its own vocabulary. ⚠️ Those vocabularies are reduced to a common set before display: without that, "hotel", "Hotel" and "Albergo" would make three rows for one thing, each with a partial count — three accurate numbers lying together.

What is set aside, and why

440,664 accommodations are marked private and appear nowhere. A private home is not an establishment: its address is a residence, and publishing it would be an intrusion, not a service. The rule does not judge case by case — it follows the source's own qualification.

Why not every place is named

Outside France a town name is not an identifier. "Jefferson County" designates some twenty distinct American counties, from one ocean to the other. A name is therefore kept as a label only if all its points fit within half a degree, about fifty-five kilometres. 163,466 accommodations — 25% — live under a name that fails this rule: they are counted in the totals, never named.

What the survey does not say

No prices, no availability, no reviews, no ranking. It says what exists and where, at a date. It does not say what it is worth, or whether it is open. ⚠️ And 40 countries have no page: their survey holds fewer than 50 accommodations, too few to carry one. They return when the harvest provides them.
