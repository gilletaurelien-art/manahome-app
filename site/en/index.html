What we do differently

  • A sovereign chain — and what isn'tInternal processing, the storage of your reports and their analysis all run on our French infrastructure. The measurement questions, however, are sent to the assistants we query — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity — whose services are operated by foreign companies: their answer is what you are buying, and there is no other way to obtain it. No confidential data is ever knowingly included in those requests.
  • An ecosystem of sources, not an isolated siteAIs cite organisations they meet repeatedly, across independent sources. We run our own — a national directory, data platforms, thematic sites — which the models have learned to consult. Making your information present and verifiable there is the heart of the work.
  • Monitoring that does not stop at the reportMost engagements end with the document. The monitoring subscription replays the same question panel every month, tracks your competitors, and alerts you when an answer turns wrong or a new name appears in your place.
  • Timelines observed, not promisedFrom what we have observed, new content takes four to twelve weeks to be taken into account, and an already well-ranked site sees its first citations sooner. These are observed tendencies, not a rule: indexing and refresh rhythms vary from one system to another, and no provider publishes them. We will tell you where you stand, with the numbers — never a guaranteed citation date.

Frequently asked questions

How soon will I see a result?

Four to twelve weeks depending on your starting point: three to eight weeks for an already well-ranked site, longer for a recent one that must build foundations first. The audit tells you which case is yours, with the numbers.

Does GEO replace traditional SEO?

No. A site invisible to Google is invisible to the assistants too, since they lean heavily on the indexed web. GEO is additive: it works on machine understanding where SEO works on ranking.

Where is my data hosted?

In France, on a dedicated OVH server at the Gravelines data centre, in the Nord département. The site, the analyses, the reports and our own analysis models all run on that one machine. The assistants we query are operated by their own publishers: American for three of them, French for Mistral — which does not, however, guarantee where it processes requests. Their answer is what we measure, and it cannot be obtained anywhere else.

We are a small organisation — is this for us?

Yes, and this is where the gap widens fastest. Start with Express at €149: you will know whether you exist for the assistants before committing to anything more.

What if an AI says something false about us?

We document it with the raw answer and its date, then trace back to the source the model used. Correcting the source is the only real lever: nobody can edit an assistant's answer.

Can I stop the monitoring?

Yes, with no notice period and no minimum term. The history of your measurements stays yours.
