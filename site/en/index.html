What this is

MANAhome publishes a survey of the world's tourist accommodation — hotels, campsites, motels, huts, farm stays — from open data. One page per country, with the types recorded, the places that can be named without error, and the share that cannot.

It is not a comparison site, a marketplace, or a paid directory. No ranking, no promotion and no placement is for sale. ⭐ That is exactly what makes the survey citable: there is nothing to buy in order to appear in it, so there is nothing to suspect.

The best surveyed

The survey is not uniform: it follows what open data covers, not the size of countries. A thinly surveyed country is not a country without accommodation — it is a country the sources describe poorly.
